Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • NetPack Logistic & NetPack Rider

NetPack Logistic ("we", "us", "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of our customers, shippers, consignees, field couriers, and dispatch riders. This Privacy Policy applies to our website (https://netpacklogistic.com), the NetPack Customer App (com.netpacklogistic.app), and the NetPack Rider Dispatch App (com.netpackpickup.pickup).

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information essential for fulfilling international air cargo, express parcel delivery, logistics tracking, and customs compliance:

  • Personal Information: Full name, email address, mobile phone number, physical pickup and delivery addresses.
  • Cargo & Package Data: Box weight, dimensions (length, breadth, height), commodity description, piece counts, declared customs valuation, and commercial invoice details.
  • Camera & Photo Uploads: Photos captured for package verification, scale weight readout proof, carton condition at pickup, and barcode/QR shipping label scanning.
  • Location Information:
    • NetPack Customer App: Approximate or precise location accessed only while actively booking a shipment to autofill your doorstep collection address.
    • NetPack Rider App: Background and foreground location data accessed during active rider duty shifts to route nearest pickup assignments, navigate to sender premises, and update customer arrival milestones.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device model, operating system version, unique application installation identifiers, network connectivity status, and crash reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data strictly for operational logistics purposes:

  • Scheduling, assigning, and executing doorstep parcel collections across Kathmandu Valley and nationwide hubs.
  • Generating Master Air Waybills (MAWB), House Air Waybills (HAWB), and customs clearance declarations for Tribhuvan International Airport (TIA) and destination ports.
  • Delivering real-time shipment status milestone updates, push notifications, and electronic delivery receipts.
  • Turn-by-turn route optimization for field couriers to reduce transit delays.
  • Preventing fraud, verifying scale weight accuracy, and protecting cargo integrity.

Zero Advertising & No Data Selling: NetPack Logistic does NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or shipment data to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing networks under any circumstances.

3. Disclosure of Data to Third Parties

Data is shared solely with trusted parties essential to completing your consignment delivery:

  • Commercial Airlines & Cargo Operators: Airline freight handlers (e.g., Nepal Airlines, Qatar Airways, Emirates SkyCargo) to manifest air transport.
  • Destination Delivery Partners: Global partner express couriers (e.g., DHL, FedEx, UPS, DPD) for destination country customs clearance and last-mile door delivery.
  • Government & Customs Authorities: Customs departments at departure and arrival ports to comply with international civil aviation and customs laws.

4. Data Security & Storage

We implement enterprise-grade technical and organizational safeguards: all data transmitted between your device and our servers is secured using modern TLS 1.3 encryption (HTTPS). Access to customer and consignment data is strictly restricted by role-based authentication controls.

5. Account Deletion & User Rights

You have full ownership of your data under applicable data protection laws. You have the right to:

  • Access, review, or update your personal account information directly within the app settings.
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

To request account deletion or data removal, email us at info@netpacklogistic.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We verify your request and securely purge non-regulatory personal records within 7 business days (records retained solely where mandated by international customs and tax laws).

6. Children's Privacy

Our services and applications are directed exclusively to adults, business shippers, and professional couriers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.
