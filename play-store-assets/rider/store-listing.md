## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

NetPack Rider is the dedicated field operations and delivery dispatch application designed exclusively for NetPack Logistic authorized riders, drivers, and field logistics executives in Nepal.

Engineered for high performance, accuracy, and ease of use, NetPack Rider connects on-ground personnel with the central logistics dispatch hub, automating pickup assignments, weight audits, and real-time status syncing.

---

### 🌟 ESSENTIAL TOOLS FOR FIELD RIDERS

⚡ INSTANT PICKUP ASSIGNMENTS & AUDIO CHIMES
Receive instantaneous pickup alerts the moment a customer requests package collection in your assigned zone. Loud distinctive chimes and haptic vibrations ensure you never miss urgent customer pickups.

🗺️ OPTIMIZED ROUTE & GPS NAVIGATION
View customer sender addresses, phone numbers, and landmarks with one-tap Google Maps integration for the fastest turn-by-turn route across Kathmandu Valley and beyond.

⚖️ ACCURATE WEIGHT & DIMENSION VERIFICATION
Verify package weight (in kg) and box dimensions (L × W × H) on-site. The app automatically calculates volumetric vs. actual weight and generates updated customer receipts instantaneously.

📸 BUILT-IN CAMERA BARCODE & PROOF SCANNER
Snap high-resolution photos of shipping labels, weight scale readouts, and customer package condition as permanent proof of pickup. Instant barcode scanning speeds up hub intake.

📊 DAILY COLLECTION SUMMARY & PERFORMANCE METRICS
Track total collected parcels, total weight handled, pending pickups, and completed deliveries over a rolling 24-hour shift with zero guesswork.

📴 OFFLINE-READY FIELD SYNC
Work seamlessly even with intermittent mobile network connections. The app automatically caches pickup logs and syncs with the central server once connectivity is restored.

---

### 📋 TYPICAL RIDER WORKFLOW:

1. **Go Online**: Switch your toggle to "Online" to begin receiving nearby pickup dispatches.
2. **Accept Pickup**: Review pickup address, box count, and navigate directly to the customer.
3. **Verify & Snap Proof**: Input scale weight, measure box dimensions, and capture proof photo.
4. **Instant Hub Sync**: Tap "Complete Pickup" to immediately notify the customer and update the central warehouse manifest.
