## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)

Welcome to NetPack Logistic — Nepal's premier international express courier, air cargo, and logistics management platform. Headquartered in Teku, Kathmandu, NetPack Logistic connects individuals, exporters, and businesses with seamless global delivery across 220+ countries worldwide.

Whether you are shipping handcrafted garments, corporate documents, e-commerce orders, or international heavy cargo, NetPack Logistic ensures rapid pickup, transparent rates, and end-to-end real-time tracking from your doorstep to destination.

---

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES & HIGHLIGHTS

📦 FAST DOORSTEP PICKUP IN KATHMANDU
Request an on-demand pickup directly from your home, workshop, or office within Kathmandu Valley. Our dispatch riders arrive promptly, verify dimensions and weight on-site, and issue immediate digital confirmation.

✈️ DIRECT AIR CARGO & 220+ COUNTRIES REACH
Enjoy direct international air cargo routes (including daily KTM ➔ DXB, Europe, UK, USA, Australia, and Asia hubs). We handle professional TIA customs documentation, clearance, and international airline handovers.

📍 REAL-TIME LIVE SHIPMENT TRACKING
Never wonder where your parcel is. Receive instantaneous milestone updates:
• Order Created & Pickup Confirmed
• Securely Weighed & Packed in Hub
• Customs Clearance & Flight Departure
• Destination Transit & Hub Scans
• Out for Delivery & Final Proof of Delivery (POD)

💰 INSTANT FREIGHT RATE CALCULATOR
Estimate transparent air freight and express courier rates before booking. Choose between standard express, heavy cargo, and priority delivery with zero hidden charges.

📄 DIGITAL RECEIPTS & COMMERCIAL INVOICES
View, download, and share detailed shipping manifests, digital waybills (HAWB/MAWB), and commercial invoices with a single tap.

🔔 TIMELY PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
Stay informed with real-time status notifications for departures, customs clearances, and safe delivery handovers.

🔒 ENTERPRISE-GRADE SECURITY & DATA PRIVACY
Built on secure, authenticated infrastructure protecting your personal consignee details, shipment contents, and account privacy.

### 🚀 HOW IT WORKS IN 3 SIMPLE STEPS:

1. **Book or Request Pickup**: Enter your pickup address and package details in seconds.
2. **Handover to Rider**: Our verified rider collects, verifies weight/dimensions, and hands you your digital receipt.
3. **Track Globally in Real Time**: Monitor your cargo’s exact flight, hub, and delivery progress until it reaches the consignee.
